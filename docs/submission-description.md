# Internship Portal Report Description

This Week 1 report presents a complete supply chain network planning exercise using SAP Supply Chain Management concepts, with emphasis on SAP APO Supply Network Planning (SNP). I created a fictional but realistic Indian food manufacturing scenario for Deccan Harvest Foods Pvt. Ltd., a company producing packaged millet-based ready-to-cook products. The network includes raw-material and packaging suppliers, a manufacturing plant, regional distribution centers, and customer markets across Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, and Karnataka. The report explains the business assumptions, stakeholder responsibilities, demand and capacity requirements, sourcing policy, inventory strategy, transportation lanes, and service-level objectives.

The SAP SCM design section documents the sequence for setting up planning-relevant master data such as locations, products/location-products, resources, production sources, transportation lanes, quota arrangements, supply chain model, planning version, planning area, and planning book. It also explains how heuristic planning can be used to build an initial feasible plan, how the SNP optimizer can evaluate cost and capacity trade-offs, and how deployment and transport-load planning can support downstream allocation and shipment consolidation.

The project includes process/network diagrams, KPI targets, configuration checklists, validation test cases, and a detailed risk register covering supplier disruption, plant downtime, demand spikes, transportation delays, packaging shortages, master-data errors, and integration issues. Contingency actions are proposed for each major risk. The final design aims to improve customer service, inventory balance, sourcing resilience, capacity visibility, and planning governance while demonstrating practical application of SAP SCM network-planning concepts.
